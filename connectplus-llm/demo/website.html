Look at who sent it

👀 What to look for

The sender's name is spelled almost right, with one letter changed. That one letter is the whole trick.

“I got a strange text about my account”

Here is what you can do

  1. Do not tap the link in the message.
  2. Look at the sender. A scam number is one you have never seen before.
  3. If you are worried, ring your bank on the number printed on your card.
  4. You can report the message to Scamwatch.

Where this comes from

SW Scamwatch — Text message scams scamwatch.gov.au/types-of-scams eS eSafety — Checking a message is genuine esafety.gov.au/seniors
